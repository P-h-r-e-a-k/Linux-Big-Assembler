# Race track planning scripts (Desert island, 2026-09-27)

The Python (numpy, scipy, scikit-image, opencv, PIL; `pip install` into a venv) that turned the picture of the proposed track into the plan the C# builder reads. They were run from `E:\dump\TEMP` and still use that folder and the picture's original path; change the paths at the top when reusing them. Method and results: [docs/LBA2_DESERT_RACE_TRACK_BUILD.md](../../docs/LBA2_DESERT_RACE_TRACK_BUILD.md).

Order: `ile.py` / `basemap.py` (read DESERT.ILE, draw the island top-down; helpers) - `landmarks.json` + `pic3.py` (register picture to island) - `pic5.py`, `pic6.py`, `pic7.py` (road skeleton, centre line by waypoints, warp to island cells) - `plan5.py` (fit the line to the real ground) - `plan_export.py` (writes `track_plan.json`, also the pit lane's two ends) - `overlay_final.py` (built route over the picture). `hshot.ps1` and `cellinfo.py` take engine screenshots of the built copy; `drive.ps1` drives the buggy (both headless and muted, on the sandbox copy).

The jump proposal: `jumpsite.py` searches the built lap for stretches straight enough for a 23-cell flight inside one cube (it leaves out the bridge, the start, the pit ends and the water bridge; writes `E:\dump\TEMP\jumpsites.json`), and `jump_proposal.py` draws `docs/racetrack/build/jump_proposal.png` from a built copy (`E:\dump\TEMP\rt4_game`, its top-down render `E:\dump\TEMP\rt4_built.png`) and the pristine island.

The race-track mode and the jump (2026-09-28): `gears.ps1` (Twinsen in the buggy on the start line, the speed while it shifts gear at set ticks, with a car setup file) and `jumpdrive.py` (drives a jump build over the jump from a parked buggy and prints the car's path through the flight). `hshot.ps1` and `drive.ps1` take `-car <file>` for the engine's race-track mode.

Cube edges (2026-09-29): `edgetest.py <game> <scratch> [island byte] [first scene] [last scene]` walks Twinsen over every place the built lap crosses a cube edge, both ways, in the engine, and reports the scene he ends in -- a crossing with no cube-change zone is an invisible wall. Citadel Island: `0 42 50`, the Desert island: `2 55 73`.

Mosquibees Island (2026-09-29), run from `E:\dump\TEMP\mosq` with the island's heights (`ScriptRoundTrip islandheights MOSQUIBE heights.csv`, turned into `H.npy`, `H[z - 448, x - 448]`) and its top-down render (`islandrender x MOSQUIBE Terrain terrain.png 4`) there. The drawing is a perspective view of the island, so `fitcam.py` fits a pinhole camera (position, turn, tilt, focal length) that turns the island's 3D ground into it, scoring the drawing's land against the land the camera would see (IoU 0.92); `perspective_trace.py` traces the drawn line (lime, the pink bridge and the blue jump) with the snake tracer and puts each point on the ground the camera sees behind that pixel. `mosquibe_design.py [deck height] [jump level] [lowest]` is the hand-made lap from that trace: control points in lap order, a closed Catmull-Rom spline every half cell, heights keyed at points and joined by straight grades with vertical curves (parabolas tangent to both grades) at every kink, the flats (start straight, bridge deck, jump) exactly level; it checks the grades, bends, the island's edge and how close the lap comes to itself, and writes `docs/racetrack/mosquibe_track_plan.json` with the bridge's and the jump's points and the pit lane. Built with `11100 12450 750`. `edgetest.py` takes `7 102 105`.

Citadel Island in the storm (2026-09-29), run from `E:\dump\TEMP\cstorm`: `citadel_storm_masks.py` (the drawing's sea, lime, pink and orange masks, and the island's heights as `H.npy`), `citadel_storm_fit.py` (the drawing is the island's map turned a quarter turn clockwise and squashed: scale and offset fitted land against land, IoU 0.84), `citadel_storm_trace.py` (the lime line skeletonised and walked into island cells, the pit lane's cells) and `citadel_storm_design.py` (the lap by hand on the ground it runs over: along the west rampart and off its end over a gap jump, down round the harbour, the pit lane and start on the east straight, a double hairpin up to the north rampart; writes `docs/racetrack/citadel_storm_track_plan.json`; `REVERSE` lays it the other way round, as it is built now). `edgetest.py` takes `0 42 50`, and `EDGE_TWIN=1` walks the town circuit (the fine-weather file's track) with `LBA2_RACETRACK_FILE` naming a car file that has `weather=fine`.

`jumptrace.py <dt ms> [car file] [look cells]` (2026-09-29): drives the built storm track (`E:\dump\TEMP\rt_cstorm`) with the test pilot at a fixed frame time with `objtrace 0` on, and reports every jump: how long Twinsen is in movement 12 (moved by his animation), when the flight animation (200) is really playing, and which animation held it off (`genanim`/`flaganim`; 2 is `ANIM_ALL_THEN`). It found the skating slide that stalled the storm track's take-off. Since 2026-09-30 it also reports each flight (its time, its length and speed along the ground, its peak) and the car's speed down the far ramp, as the engine now flies a jump at the car's speed; `jumptrace.py <engine log>` gives the same report from any track's run (Mosquibees Island's, say, from `E:\dump\TEMP\elev\pilot.sh` with `GAME`/`SCENE`/`CAR` set). `SIMSTEP=<ms>` steps the simulation at that too (the engine steps at 16 ms whatever the frame time), `JT_SHOTS=<tick>,...` takes screenshots, `JT_TAG` gives a run a folder of its own, `JT_LOG` keeps the log.

Celebration Island (2026-09-30), run from `E:\dump\TEMP\celeb`: the lap winds up round the statue on a raised road and comes back down a bridge, so the design needs the statue itself, which is decor and not ground. `ScriptRoundTrip decorpoints CELEBRA2 decorpoints.csv` (with `LBA2_DIR` the game folder) writes every decor body's points and, as `decorpoints.faces.csv`, its triangles in island coordinates; `celebration_mesh.py` reads them (a point cloud of the statue and the temple, which bodies reach into a given space, orthographic renders of the island with the lap). `celebration_design.py [--pictures]` is the lap: the dock's start straight, two rounded-rectangle loops at one grade up to the statue's shoulders, a level ring round the head, a straight bridge with parabolic ends back to the dock, a quay hairpin; it checks the grade, the height between parts of the lap that lie over each other, the cube's edge, and what decor the road's space touches, and writes `docs/racetrack/celebration_track_plan.json` with the raised span, the road's widths, the start point and the opponents' waiting spots. Built with `RT_ISLAND="Celebration Island" ScriptRoundTrip buildtrack ...`.

`raisedtest.py <game> <car file> <scratch> [scenario ...]` (2026-09-30): the raised road in the engine. It puts Twinsen's car, or Twinsen on foot, on a stretch of the road in a headless run of the race-track mode, holds some keys (ahead, hard left and right, reverse, up the bridge, across the road), and checks from `objtrace` that he stays at the road's height and inside its rails. The road is read from the file the car file's `raised=` line names (`ScriptRoundTrip racecarfile <game> <car file>` writes both).

The Elevator Platform (2026-09-30), run from `E:\dump\TEMP\elev` with the island's heights (`H.npy`) and `decorpoints.faces.csv` from `ScriptRoundTrip decorpoints ASCENCE` there. `island_mesh.py` is `celebration_mesh.py` for any island (the folder from `ISLAND_MESH_DIR`). `elevator_design.py [--pictures]` is the rollercoaster: a station level with the main deck in front of the elevator's door, a helix round the tower (`ELEV_TURNS` whole turns and a quarter, levels `ELEV_PITCH` apart), a drop (`ELEV_DROP`, its grade) into a diving turn, a camelback, a hill under the drop, the airship, three hops; the banking from the curvature (`ELEV_BANK`); it checks the height between parts of the lap over each other (cross-section against cross-section), the cube's edge and what of the platform's real shape is in the road's space (the banked surface counted), and writes `docs/racetrack/elevator_track_plan.json` with `bank`, `gravity`, `railCamera` (`ELEV_CAMERA`: cells behind, units up, cells ahead) and `pierBodies`. Built with `RT_ISLAND="Elevator Platform" ScriptRoundTrip buildtrack ...`; `raisedtest.py` takes `RAISED_SCENE=120`.

`builtviews.py <game> <ISLAND> <scratch> <first new body> [west|east|south|north|top|iso ...] [--scale s] [--top height]` (2026-09-30): pictures of a built island with every decor body, a raised road's pieces and piers among them, without the engine. `pilottrace.py <engine log> <raised road file> [cube x] [cube z]`: from a headless run of the test pilot with `objtrace 0`, the car's speed along the lap, where the rail holds it and how far it is ever off the road's height; and, frame by frame, whether it ever goes under the road it was on or past the rail (what an opponent's car pushing against it did before 2026-09-30's fix).

Several tracks in one folder (2026-09-30): `ScriptRoundTrip buildtogether <pristine> <game> <island>...` builds the islands' built-in tracks together, as the race track window does with several ticked; `RT_RACE=<island file> ScriptRoundTrip racecarfile <game> <car file>` writes the car file Play would with that island open (give each its own folder: the opponents' line files are written beside it). `jumptrace.py` takes `JT_GAME=<folder>` to drive the storm track of another build (the storm track has no opponents, so its test pilot needs a line of its own: `opponent_path=E:\dump\TEMP\cstorm\racepath_storm.txt` added to the car file), and counts any of the flights (200-295). The command line's builds take each island's own crossing (`RaceTrackIsland.Crossing`: the Desert island's jump, the town circuit's bridge) as the window does; `RT_CROSSING` overrides it.

Celebration Island before the statue (2026-10-01), run from `E:\dump\TEMP\celebrat` (the cube's heights and game codes as `H.npy` and `code.npy`, made from the untouched `CELEBRAT.ILE` when missing): `celebrat_design.py [--pictures]` is the lava lake lap -- a figure of eight given as corners and radii (a polyline with its corners rounded), the crossing of its two causeways worked out and a gap jump on each (lips 4.5 cells either side of it), heights from the ground under the road held level through the jumps and kept within `WALL_FREE` of the ground under the road's middle (raised over a knoll, never cut into it: the builder walls a planned road further than 1,200 from the ground), the start line and the waiting spots; it checks the straight each leap needs, the temple, the lap against itself and the walls, and writes `docs/racetrack/celebrat_track_plan.json` with `gapJumps` and the jumps' sizes. Built with `buildtogether <pristine> <game> "Celebration Island, lava lake"`; the test pilot drives it with `cube 223` and a car file from `RT_RACE=CELEBRAT.ILE racecarfile`.
