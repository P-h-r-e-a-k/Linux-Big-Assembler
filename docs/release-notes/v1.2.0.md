Three weeks of LBAAssembler, and the biggest release yet: two new islands made from LBA1, maps you can
save and share as files of their own, a race track you draw with a pen, and the scene editing moved into
the 3D view where you can see what you are changing.

## New islands

- **The Temple of Bú** (Tools > LBA2: Temple of Bú): LBA1's temple — all three of its scenes — ported into
  LBA2 as an island of its own, in LBA1's own colours, so from LBA1's angle it looks the way LBA1 drew it.
  Its bricks become real 3D: 1,951 bodies, and 85 of LBA1's own actors (skeletons, spike traps, floor
  plates, lift platforms, the Book of Bù) come with it. The race track window builds its Halloween race.
- **The Snow island** (Tools > LBA2: Snow island): a new island the size of the Desert island, in the
  spirit of LBA1's Hamalayi Mountains — ice cliffs and terraces, a canyon up to the prison gate, a frozen
  pond, the Rabbibunnies' village, a ski slope with its chair lift, snowmen. It is saved as a map of its
  own and opened straight away to explore and Play. It needs your LBA1 folder for the bricks.
- **Otringal's palace** and **Island CX's control tower**, both as islands of their own in 3D.

## Maps as files of their own

**File > New map / Open map / Save map / Install map into game / Export map.** An island or an interior can
now live in a `.lbamap` file outside your game folder: made from one of the game's own, edited and played,
then installed into the game as new scenes when you are ready. **Map settings** names it and sets an
island's weather; **Map text** holds its own lines of dialogue.

**File > Import / Export heightmap** shapes an island's ground from a greyscale picture — it tells you what
size it needs, shows the result live in the 3D view, and it is one undo step.

## Editing the scene in the view

**Build > Actors and zones** is now a panel beside the 3D view instead of a separate window: the scene's
actors, zones and track points are picked, dragged and placed in the view itself, where you can see the
ground they land on. The two old scene editor windows are gone — this replaces them.

A **character maker** builds a character of the map's own, from one of the game's bodies or from nothing,
with its animations keyframe by keyframe; an **object maker** does the same for the things on an island.

## The race track generator

**Tools > LBA2: race track generator** draws a track rather than writing one: a pen for the road, pinks for
jumps, blues for loops, orange for the pit lane, on the island's own map. Or place track sections that snap
together — straights, hairpins, spirals, ramps. Or save the map as a picture, draw on it in any paint
program and load it back. The lap is turned into a real track with its racing line and a height profile you
can drag into shape, then handed to the race track window to build.

New tracks built in: **Otringal** (through the game's tunnel, the town and round the palace), **Volcano
Island** (over the lava), **Wannies Island**, the **Palace**, the **control tower** and the **Temple**.

## Racing

- **The minimap goes over to the race while you play one**: the lap fitted to its box, the road drawn over
  the island, and every car where the engine has it a few times a second — Twinsen's yellow, each opponent
  a colour of its own.
- **Twinsen's laser cars**: his buggy with its gun firing green or red.
- The race items now have **sounds** of their own as they are picked up and used.
- Volcano Island's lava falls, crystals and boost panel; Otringal's tunnel, machine gun, roulette wheel,
  gas monsters and hoop.

## Also

- **Sea/gas** beside Sky in the viewport toolbar hides the sea or gas round an island, to see what is under
  it — the control tower's halls under Island CX's gas.

## Fixed on Linux

- **The editor can talk to the running game again.** The engine shipped in 1.0.0 to 1.1.1 was built without
  its control socket, so script breakpoints, the embedded window's live resolution, the sound levels and
  the new race minimap were all silently unavailable. They work now. (The engine still opens no socket
  unless the editor starts it with `--listen`, and it binds to this machine only.)
- **Cancel and Close did nothing** in the race car setup and the race track window: WPF closes a dialog by
  its Cancel button on its own and Avalonia does not.
- The "keep my data here" setting now moves the temporary folder on Linux too (.NET reads `TMPDIR`, not
  `TEMP`).

## Known issue

**Tools > LBA1: Make surprise changes** stops the editor with "Life decompiler could not produce
round-trippable text". It comes from a change upstream made to how a script's labels are looked up while
another script is being checked, it bites on LBA1 scene 24, and it is not specific to Linux. Everything
else in the LBA1 editor is unaffected.
